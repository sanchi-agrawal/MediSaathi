adaptations by MediSaathi: PBR material colors, shared upper-body framing,

These adapted model representations are also offered under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.
This notice does not relicense unrelated application code.

Brain, lungs, heart, liver, stomach, kidneys and intestines are interactive
when their assets load. The original organ GLB supplies the cerebellum, pons
and medulla. Those original parts are reused with the added real cortex.

anatomy-cerebrum.glb is an adaptation of 37 original BodyParts3D 3.0 cortical
STL components (bilateral gyri, parietal/occipital regions, insula and medial
cortical parts) from:
https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D/tree/main/assets/BodyParts3D_data/stl
Source creator: The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan.
Source license: CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan; the adapted GLB uses the same license.
Exact per-component source URLs, pinned blob hashes, names and the recovered
coordinate matrix are in brain-sources.json alongside this notice.
Changes by MediSaathi: one common axis conversion/uniform scale/translation
registered to matching stomach, kidney and pons; welded vertices, smoothed
normals, bounded simplification, merged draw call and Draco compression.
The cortex is original anatomical geometry, not a procedural approximation.
Rebuild: python scripts/fetch-brain-sources.py then node scripts/build-brain.mjs

The outer skin is the matching BodyParts3D skin__FMA7163 mesh in its source
coordinate system. No body geometry or major vessel layer was fabricated.
